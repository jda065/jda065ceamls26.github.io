what_i_learned: |
 Today was our first day meeting in person rather than over Zoom, which gave everyone an opportunity to connect face-to-face and become more familiar with one another. We reviewed the expectations for the program and discussed what will be required of us throughout the remainder of the institute. We also participated in a workshop covering NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib, where we learned how these tools are used for data analysis, data manipulation, and data visualization. The workshop helped reinforce many of the concepts we have been learning throughout the summer and showed how these tools can be applied to real datasets. In addition, we took part in a team-building activity where we worked together to build a tower capable of balancing a marshmallow. The activity encouraged collaboration, communication, and creative problem-solving while allowing us to bond with our peers.
blockers: |
 One challenge today was adjusting from a virtual environment to an in-person setting and staying focused while balancing both the technical workshop and group activities. During the workshop, some of the concepts involving data manipulation and visualization moved quickly, so I occasionally needed extra time to process how the different libraries work together. However, working alongside my peers helped make the material easier to understand.
reflection: |
 Overall, today was a great experience and a refreshing change from meeting virtually. It was nice to finally meet everyone in person and strengthen the relationships we have built throughout the program. I enjoyed the workshop because it connected many of the Python concepts we have learned to practical data science applications. The marshmallow tower challenge was also a fun way to practice teamwork and problem-solving while taking a break from the technical material. By the end of the day, I felt more connected to the group and even more excited about the work we will continue to do throughout the institute.
