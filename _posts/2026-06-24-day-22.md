what_i_learned: |
 Today we participated in a data analysis workshop where we expanded our understanding of how data is prepared and analyzed for machine learning applications. We learned how to sort, clean, and manipulate datasets so they can be used more effectively for analysis. We also discussed the importance of splitting data into training and testing datasets and how machine learning algorithms use these datasets to learn patterns and evaluate model performance. In addition to the technical workshop, we attended a mock interview session where we learned what recruiters typically look for in candidates, common interview mistakes to avoid, and strategies for presenting ourselves professionally. The session provided valuable insight into both the technical and professional skills needed to succeed in future internships and career opportunities.

blockers: |
 One challenge I experienced today was maintaining focus throughout the length of the workshop while processing a large amount of new technical information. I also found it difficult to stay fully engaged at times because the communication style used by certain leaders during portions of the session felt more critical than constructive. I learn best in environments where feedback is delivered with professionalism, respect, and encouragement, and I believe that a more considerate approach to communication would create a more positive learning experience. Moving forward, I want to continue focusing on the material itself while making the most of every learning opportunity.

reflection: |
 Overall, today was a productive and worthwhile experience. I enjoyed learning more about the machine learning workflow and seeing how data preparation directly impacts the performance of predictive models. The mock interview workshop was also valuable because it provided practical advice that I can use when applying for internships and future career opportunities. Every session continues to strengthen both my technical knowledge and my professional development. I am excited to keep building these skills, applying them to my research project, and becoming more confident as both a programmer and a researcher.
