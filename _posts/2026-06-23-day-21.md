 Today I expanded my Python data analysis skills by learning how to sort and organize complex datasets, an essential step in preparing data for meaningful analysis. I applied these techniques to a dataset focused on asthma risk factors in Baltimore City and Baltimore County, sorting the information to better identify neighborhoods with the highest and lowest levels of risk. This process made it easier to recognize geographic trends and potential environmental disparities. In addition to data sorting, I learned how to interpret box plots and understand the information they communicate. I can now identify the median, upper and lower quartiles, overall distribution, and outliers within a dataset. Understanding box plots allows me to quickly compare asthma-related risk factors across different communities and recognize areas that may require further investigation. Combining these visualization techniques with data organization has strengthened my ability to analyze and interpret public health data in a more meaningful way.
