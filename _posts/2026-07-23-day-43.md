what_i_learned: |
 Today our team presented our final demonstration in front of the program directors as a practice run. During the presentation, we realized we were not fully prepared and had to stop before completing it. Although it was disappointing, the experience gave us valuable feedback on what needed improvement. For the rest of the day, we focused on practicing our presentation, refining our transitions, improving our timing, and making sure each team member was confident in their speaking role. We also worked on strengthening our delivery so that our research could be presented more clearly and professionally when we were given another opportunity to present the following morning.

blockers: |
 Our biggest challenge today was realizing that we were not fully prepared during our initial practice presentation. We needed to improve our organization, timing, and confidence so that the presentation flowed naturally. Coordinating multiple speakers while ensuring smooth transitions also required additional practice and communication among the team.

reflection: |
 Although today's presentation did not go as planned, it became an important learning experience for our team. Receiving another opportunity to present the next morning motivated us to focus on improving rather than dwelling on the setback. Spending the rest of the day rehearsing helped build our confidence and strengthened our teamwork. It reminded me that preparation, communication, and adaptability are just as important as the research itself. By the end of the day, I felt much more prepared and optimistic about delivering a successful final presentation.
