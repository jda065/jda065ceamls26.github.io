 Today I worked on developing my slides for our weekly presentation and organizing the results from my machine learning analysis. I focused on presenting my balanced and unbalanced cross-validation results in a clear way and explaining how data balancing can affect model performance. I also conducted additional research on class weighting and how it can be applied to my asthma dataset. Class weighting assigns a greater penalty to errors made on the minority class, encouraging the model to pay more attention to underrepresented asthma cases without creating synthetic data like SMOTE. I explored how class weighting could be applied to my models and compared with my previous balancing methods.
