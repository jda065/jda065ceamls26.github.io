what_i_learned: |
 Today our team recorded the video for our 90-second elevator pitch, which we decided to present as a news broadcast. We focused on developing dialogue that was engaging, natural, and informative while clearly communicating our research project and its impact. After filming, I spent the majority of the day editing the video in CapCut. I added transitions, organized clips, searched for additional footage to strengthen the story, and worked on making the overall video flow more smoothly. Throughout the editing process, I became much more familiar with CapCut and learned several new editing techniques that improved the quality of our final product.

blockers: |
 One challenge I faced today was finding the right video clips and visuals that matched our script while maintaining a professional presentation. Another challenge was learning some of CapCut's editing features, since I was still becoming familiar with the software. It also took time to ensure that the dialogue, transitions, and visuals were synchronized so the news skit felt natural and engaging.

reflection: |
 Overall, today was a fun and creative change of pace from coding and data analysis. I enjoyed collaborating with my teammates to bring our idea to life through a news-style presentation. Spending time editing the video allowed me to develop new multimedia skills while thinking about how to communicate technical research to a broader audience. By the end of the day, I felt much more comfortable using CapCut and was proud of the progress we made on our presentation. I'm excited to put the finishing touches on the video and share the final product.
