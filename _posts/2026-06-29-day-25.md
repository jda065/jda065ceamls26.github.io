 Today was our final presentation day. Our group presented first, which made the experience both exciting and nerve-racking. We presented the research we had been working on throughout the program, explained our data analysis process, discussed our findings, and answered questions from the audience. Although our demonstration video experienced technical difficulties and was unable to play as intended, we adapted by explaining what the video was meant to demonstrate and continued with the presentation. I also learned the importance of being prepared for unexpected challenges and communicating confidently even when things do not go according to plan.
blockers: |
 One challenge I faced today was managing my nerves before presenting, especially since our group was first. Despite being nervous, I was able to communicate my section and contribute to answering questions from the audience. Another challenge was the technical issue with our demonstration video, which required us to adjust our presentation on the spot. Later in the day, I found it difficult to stay fully attentive and engaged while watching several presentations in a row, although I still made an effort to listen and learn from the other groups' work.

 Overall, today was a great way to conclude the first half of theprogram. I am proud of how our team worked together and handled unexpected challenges professionally.
