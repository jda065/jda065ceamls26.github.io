 Today I shifted my focus back to the results section of my research paper. I began organizing and incorporating the findings from each member of our team into a single, cohesive section.  My biggest challenge today was efficiently integrating everyone's findings into the results section while maintaining a logical flow. I also continued experimenting with my Douglas algorithm, which required testing and fine-tuning to identify additional opportunities for optimization.

 Today reminded me that completing a research project involves much more than building machine learning models. Collaborating with my teammates to clarify their findings helped ensure our results section accurately reflected the work we completed throughout the program. At the same time, continuing to optimize my Douglas algorithm allowed me to strengthen the technical side of my project. By the end of the day, I felt that both my research paper and my machine learning model had made meaningful progress toward completion.
