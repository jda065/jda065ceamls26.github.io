what_i_learned: |
  Today we went through a walkthrough of our timesheets and learned how to properly complete and submit them. Since my WebSis documentation was not yet available online, I had to complete two paper versions of the timesheet form. One of our teammates was absent, so I created a short instructional video using CapCut to help demonstrate the process of filling out the form. After completing our timesheets, we reviewed our Overleaf documents and made several edits and formatting changes that were requested. We also participated in a game of Taboo, where we had to describe words using limited clues. The activity helped strengthen our communication and teamwork skills. After lunch, we began cleaning and sorting data from an environmental dataset. My focus was on asthma-related variables and identifying which data points would be most useful for analysis. At the end of the day, we came together as a group to compare our approaches and discuss the different methods we used to organize and filter the data.

blockers: |
  One challenge I faced today was working through the timesheet process without having access to my online WebSis documentation. Completing the paper forms required extra attention to make sure everything was accurate. It also sucked because I could not directly follow along as it was being shown. Another challenge was determining which variables in the environmental dataset were most relevant to asthma and deciding what information should be kept or removed during the data-cleaning process.

reflection: |
  Overall, today was a productive and enjoyable day. I appreciated the variety of activities we worked on. The Taboo activity was a fun way to strengthen communication and teamwork while taking a break from our technical work. I especially enjoyed working with the environmental dataset because it gave me an opportunity to apply what I have been learning to a real research problem related to asthma. Comparing my methods with those of my teammates showed me that there are often multiple ways to approach the same task and reinforced the importance of collaboration. By the end of the day, I felt more confident in my ability to work with data and contribute to our research project. I am looking forward to continuing our analysis and seeing how our work develops in the coming weeks.
