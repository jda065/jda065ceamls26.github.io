what_i_learned: |
  Today a small group of us got together to celebrate the successful completion of the summer research program. We spent time bowling, playing arcade games, taking photos, and reflecting on everything we accomplished over the past several weeks. Although the day was centered around having fun, it reminded me of the importance of building relationships with the people you work alongside. Throughout the program, we supported one another through challenges, celebrated each other's successes, and grew both academically and personally. Taking time to celebrate together showed me that the connections made during a research experience can be just as meaningful as the technical skills gained.

blockers: |
  My biggest challenge today was that I didn't want the celebration to end—I could have kept bowling for hours. More importantly, it was bittersweet knowing that the program had officially come to an end. After spending so much time learning, researching, and working alongside everyone, it was difficult to say goodbye and realize that this chapter was over.

reflection: |
  Today was the perfect way to conclude an unforgettable summer. While the arcade games, bowling, and photos were enjoyable, what I will remember most are the friendships, mentorship, and experiences that came from the program. Looking back, I can see how much I have grown as a researcher, student, and communicator. Although it is sad to see the program end, I am grateful for everything I learned and excited to apply these experiences to future research, engineering opportunities, and my academic career. The end of the program feels less like a finish line and more like the beginning of the next stage of my journey.
