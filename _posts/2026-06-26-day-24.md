what_i_learned: |
 Today we finalized our presentation by assembling all of our slides into a clear and organized format. As a team, we reviewed the flow of the presentation, assigned speaking roles, and decided who would present each section. We completed several mock runs to improve our transitions and ensure everyone was comfortable with their assigned material. After making revisions, we received feedback and approval from our graduate mentors, which gave us confidence that our presentation was on the right track. We also presented in front of two other research groups, giving us another opportunity to practice our delivery, receive constructive feedback, and identify areas for improvement before the final presentation.
blockers: |
 One challenge I faced today was managing my nerves while presenting in front of others. Sometimes I become anxious when speaking, which can make it more difficult to clearly organize my thoughts and communicate my ideas. Another challenge was making sure our presentation stayed within the required time limit. Since we have a lot of information to share, we had to carefully balance providing enough detail while remaining concise and staying on schedule.
reflection: |
 Overall, today was a very productive and rewarding day. It was exciting to see our presentation come together after weeks of research, coding, and data analysis. Practicing in front of other groups helped me build confidence and gave our team valuable feedback that we can use to improve before the final presentation. Although I still get nervous when speaking in front of an audience, each practice session makes me feel more prepared and comfortable. I am proud of how much our team has accomplished and appreciate how everyone worked together to create a strong presentation. I am looking forward to presenting our final project and showcasing everything we have learned throughout the program.
