what_i_learned: |
 Today we attended the AWS Convention, where we had the opportunity to hear from several keynote speakers working in technology and cloud computing. The speakers shared their career journeys, discussed emerging trends in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and innovation, and emphasized the importance of continuous learning and adaptability in the technology industry. One topic that stood out was Agentic AI, which refers to AI systems that can make decisions, plan tasks, and complete objectives with minimal human intervention. Learning about how AI agents are beginning to automate complex workflows gave me a better understanding of where artificial intelligence is headed and how it may transform industries in the future. Throughout the convention, I also gained a better understanding of the wide range of career opportunities available in technology and how skills in programming, data analysis, and machine learning can be applied in real-world settings.

blockers: |
 One challenge I faced today was staying focused during some of the longer keynote sessions, as there was a large amount of information presented in a short period of time. Another challenge was processing all of the new technologies and concepts, especially topics like Agentic AI, while also thinking about how they relate to my own academic interests and future career goals.

reflection: |
 Overall, today was an inspiring experience. Attending the AWS Convention allowed me to see how the concepts we have been learning throughout the program are being applied at the highest levels of the technology industry. Hearing professionals discuss innovations such as cloud computing and Agentic AI motivated me to continue strengthening my programming, data analysis, and machine learning skills. The event also reinforced the importance of networking, lifelong learning, and staying adaptable as technology continues to evolve. I left the convention feeling inspired and excited about the opportunities available in engineering and artificial intelligence, and it encouraged me to continue working toward a career where I can contribute to these rapidly advancing fields.
