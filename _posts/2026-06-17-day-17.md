what_i_learned: |
  Today we worked in our programming groups to share and compare the progress we had made on our projects. Maya and I created a video presentation, while Chris and Chanel worked on their own video. Afterward, we came together as a group to discuss our work, combine ideas, and learn from each other's approaches. We also spent time discussing literature reviews and their importance in supporting research projects. Each pair was assigned a research article to review, and we analyzed the studies to better understand their purpose, methodology, findings, and limitations. This helped strengthen my ability to read and evaluate research papers while identifying information that may be useful for our own project.
blockers: |
  One challenge I faced today was staying fully engaged while other groups were presenting, especially during longer discussions. Another blocker was continuing to work on consolidating my writing so that my ideas are clear, concise, and easy to follow. I also found it challenging to identify the target variables within some of the research studies we reviewed. Determining exactly what outcome the researchers were trying to predict or analyze required careful reading and interpretation of the articles.
reflection: |
  Overall, today was a fun and productive day. I enjoyed collaborating with my teammates and seeing the different approaches each group took when presenting their work. The literature review activities helped me become more comfortable analyzing research papers and identifying key components of a study. While there are still areas I want to improve, especially with technical writing and interpreting research variables, I can see my skills developing over time. I look forward to continuing to build on these skills and applying them to our research project moving forward.
