what_i_learned: |
  Today was the culmination of the summer research program as every team presented their work to faculty, mentors, fellow participants, and guests. My team successfully delivered our final presentation, sharing our research on using machine learning and optimization techniques to predict asthma risk using urban environmental data. Compared to our earlier rehearsals, I felt much more confident and prepared, and I was able to communicate our research clearly. After presenting, I listened to the presentations from the other research teams, which exposed me to a variety of projects and approaches across different STEM disciplines. Hearing how each group addressed unique research questions helped me appreciate the diversity of scientific research and the many ways data, engineering, and technology can be used to solve real-world problems. The day concluded with an awards ceremony, group photos, and a celebration of everyone's accomplishments throughout the program.

The awards ceremony and group photos gave me time to reflect on everything our cohort accomplished together over the summer. I finished the program feeling proud of my team's work, grateful for the support of the mentors and faculty, and motivated to continue pursuing research and engineering opportunities in the future.
