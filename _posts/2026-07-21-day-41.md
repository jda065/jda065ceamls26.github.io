what_i_learned: |
 Today I focused on preparing for the conclusion of the research program by working on both my final presentation and my final research paper. Since my primary area of research is asthma prediction, I spent time compiling and organizing all of my model results so they could be presented clearly in both my paper and presentation. I also reviewed my evaluation metrics and compared my models to determine which results best demonstrated the effectiveness of my approach. In addition to my research, I brainstormed ideas for our demonstration video, thinking about the best way to communicate our project's purpose, methodology, and findings in an engaging and informative format. Throughout the day, I learned that presenting research effectively requires careful organization and clear storytelling just as much as strong technical results.

blockers: |
 My biggest challenge today was that several of my machine learning models were returning **NaN** values instead of producing valid evaluation metrics. Because of this, I was unable to draw definitive conclusions from some of my experiments and had to spend additional time troubleshooting the models to identify the source of the problem. This slowed my progress on both the paper and presentation since I wanted to ensure I was reporting accurate and reliable results.

reflection: |
 Today highlighted how important it is to verify results before including them in a research paper or presentation. While encountering NaN values was frustrating, it reminded me that debugging and validating models are essential parts of the research process. I was still able to make meaningful progress by organizing my asthma results, improving my presentation materials, and brainstorming ideas for our demo video. As the project comes to an end, I feel more confident not only in my technical abilities but also in my ability to communicate my research effectively. My next step is to resolve the remaining model issues so that I can confidently present my final findings.
