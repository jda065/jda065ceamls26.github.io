what_i_learned: |
 Today we worked on our data exploration and preprocessing assignment using our individual research datasets. My focus was on the asthma dataset, where I analyzed the data to better understand its structure before building machine learning models. We explored concepts such as skewness, class balance, feature correlation, variance, multicollinearity using Variance Inflation Factor (VIF), feature importance, and train-test splitting. We also discussed why preprocessing steps such as standardization and SMOTE are important for improving model performance and reducing bias. Throughout the assignment, I learned that preparing data is just as important as building the model because the quality of the data directly affects the accuracy and reliability of the results.
blockers: |
 One of the biggest challenges I faced today was interpreting the results rather than simply generating them. Understanding why certain variables were highly skewed, why some features had stronger correlations than others, and deciding whether preprocessing techniques should be applied required careful analysis. I also spent time making sure I could justify each preprocessing decision instead of only relying on the output from the code.
reflection: |
 Overall, today helped strengthen my understanding of the complete machine learning workflow. Instead of focusing only on writing code, I had to think critically about why each preprocessing step is necessary and how it impacts the final model. Working through my asthma dataset gave me a much better appreciation for the importance of cleaning, balancing, and preparing data before training a model. Although some of the concepts required additional thought, I feel much more confident in my ability to interpret data and explain my decisions. I am excited to continue building on these skills as we move closer to developing and evaluating our predictive models.
