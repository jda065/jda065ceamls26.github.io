what_i_learned: |
 Today I focused on analyzing the results from my Random Forest, SVM, and Extreme Learning Machine models for predicting asthma. I compared RMSE and MAE values between the training and testing data to determine how well each model performed on unseen data. I also analyzed balanced versus imbalanced datasets to see how SMOTE affected the models and whether balancing the data improved prediction performance. In addition, I reviewed K-fold cross-validation results, which allowed me to compare model performance across multiple sections of the dataset instead of relying on one train-test split. Based on my analysis, ELM is currently showing the strongest performance for predicting asthma outcomes.
blockers: |
 One challenge I faced was interpreting the large number of results produced from the different models and evaluation methods. Comparing balanced and imbalanced data, K-fold results, and  RMSE and MAE values required careful attention. I had to focus on understanding why the results changed instead of only identifying the lowest error score.
reflection: |
 Overall, today helped me become more confident in analyzing machine learning results. Comparing balanced and imbalanced data showed me how preprocessing can affect model performance, while K-fold cross-validation helped me understand how consistently a model performs on unseen data. I enjoyed seeing the differences between each model and using the results to support why ELM is currently my strongest model for predicting asthma.
