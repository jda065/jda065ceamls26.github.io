what_i_learned: |
  Today I attended the National Symposium on Effective and Ethical AI, where I had the opportunity to learn from researchers, educators, students, and industry professionals working in different areas of artificial intelligence. Throughout the day, I attended presentations and discussions that explored responsible AI development, human-AI collaboration, research innovation, and the use of AI to address real-world problems. Listening to experienced researchers explain their methods, findings, and challenges helped me better understand how professional research is conducted and presented. The symposium also showed me that developing a successful AI system involves considering fairness, transparency, ethics, and its possible effects on society.

blockers: |
  One challenge was understanding some of the advanced topics discussed during the presentations. The speakers covered a wide range of research areas, so I had to listen carefully, take notes, and connect the information to concepts I learned during the summer program. Processing so much new information in one day was difficult, but it encouraged me to ask questions and think more critically about artificial intelligence.

reflection: |
  Today was an inspiring learning experience because it allowed me to see how researchers and professionals are applying AI across different fields. The symposium helped me recognize that the machine-learning skills I developed during the summer are connected to a much larger research community. It also reinforced the importance of creating AI systems that are effective, ethical, and beneficial to society. I left the event with a greater appreciation for AI research and more motivation to continue developing my technical, communication, and critical-thinking skills.
