what_i_learned: |
  Today I attended the National Symposium on Effective and Ethical AI, where I had the opportunity to learn from researchers, educators, and industry professionals working across a wide range of AI applications. I attended keynote presentations and research sessions that explored topics such as human-AI collaboration, AI governance, responsible AI, scientific discovery, and interdisciplinary AI innovation. Listening to researchers explain not only their results but also their methodology, challenges, and future work gave me a better understanding of what it means to conduct and communicate impactful research. The symposium also exposed me to new perspectives on how AI can be developed responsibly while addressing real-world problems. Overall, the experience broadened my understanding of the AI research community and showed me how the work we completed during the summer fits into a much larger field of innovation.

blockers: |
  One challenge today was processing the large amount of information presented throughout the symposium. Many of the talks covered advanced research topics, so I had to actively take notes and connect the concepts to my own machine learning project. I also challenged myself to think critically about how the ideas presented could be applied to my future research.

reflection: |
  Today was one of the most inspiring experiences of the program. Hearing accomplished researchers discuss their work reinforced the importance of developing AI systems that are not only effective but also ethical, transparent, and beneficial to society. It was motivating to see the different ways AI is being used across disciplines and to recognize that the skills I developed this summer are part of a much larger research ecosystem. I left the symposium with a greater appreciation for research, stronger motivation to continue learning, and new ideas that I hope to apply in future projects.
